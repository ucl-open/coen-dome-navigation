## Decided

- Initial names (Pip, 20 Nov 2026): every stimulus setting says it is the value as the trial starts, since any may one day change within a trial. In the task file `visualStimulus.contrasts` is `initialContrasts`, `spatialFrequencyStartCpd` is `initialSpatialFrequencyCpd`, `spatialFrequencyEndCpd` is `trackEndSpatialFrequencyCpd`, and `auditoryStimulus.levelDbfs` and `elevationDeg` are `initialLevelDbfs` and `initialElevationDeg` (lib-open-rigs' `ct.AuditoryStimulus`); the pips' default is -45 in task.py too. Trial columns put the modality first, then the timing word: `visualInitialContrast`, `auditoryInitialLevelDbfs`, `auditoryInitialElevationDeg`, `auditoryOutcomeFrequencyHz`, `visualOutcomeSpatialFrequencyCpd`. WorkflowData_StimulusState keeps `auditoryLevelDbfs` and `visualContrast` (each pip's instantaneous value). A clean break: old keys are refused; jacuzzi-ingest maps old names when converting recorded data.
- The hardware is lib-open-rigs' `DomeHardware`; this workflow keeps its own task file reading, task C# (`src\Extensions\TrialLogic.cs`, the rules lib-open-rigs' `TrialLoop` runs, tested in `tests\Extensions.Tests`, every wait on the Harp clock) and `WorkflowData_StimulusState`. What every task does the same way is lib's (`TrialLoop`, and the includes `ReadLabRig`, `LaunchSettings`, `BackgroundNoise`, `PlayPenaltySound`, `TaskLogs`, `LogRngSeed`, `ExperimentRecords`, `ConnectForTask`, `Rewards`); the pieces declare their own commands and parameters, so ExperimentControl declares none.
- The track's tone runs 2 to 24 kHz, as Tom's workflow did (Pip, 16 Nov 2026): his 'cm to Hz', and drift divided `endHz / startHz` as whole numbers (25000 / 2000 = 12), so his mice learned the reward zones there (15248 Hz at 81.7% of the track). The 13 Nov rebuild had made it 2 to 25 kHz, moving every zone about 1.5% of the track earlier; `Sweep.TrackRatio` restores it. The tone files still span 2 to 25 kHz, so the file row uses the exact ratio, as Tom's did.
- The background noise is the lab's one file, rig.yml's `speakerArray.backgroundNoise` (`../shared/sounds/background_noise.wav`, this repo's old float WAV byte for byte; Pip: "It should be literally the same file"), from Ready, faded in over 2 s, unequalised, with no task file block or level (the build removed it).
- Every sound's level is the task file's `levelDbfs` (dBFS RMS as stored, before equalisation; the pips' `initialLevelDbfs`): the pips -45 (`auditoryStimulus.initialLevelDbfs`; Pip, 20 Nov 2026, was -55), the reward tone -45 (`reward.tone.levelDbfs`; Pip, 20 Nov 2026, was -55) and the penalty sound -35 (`penalty.incorrect.sound.levelDbfs`, `penalty.noResponse.sound.levelDbfs`), per condition; the pip files are stored at -58.1 (`sweep.json`'s pipLevelDbfs, below); the background noise is the lab's file with no task file level. The penalty sound is the lab's, on rig.yml's `penaltySoundChannel`; the pips and reward tone play on rig.yml's speakers, each equalised.
- `sweep.json` is read at Start (`LoadSweep`), with the rate the waveforms were made at, checked against rig.yml's mixer rate.
- The quiet waits: `lickQuietSeconds` per stage, `wheelQuietSeconds` and `wheelQuietCm` in `trialStructure` (no stage changes them), capped at 10 s, 10 s and 20 cm; the wheel's counts travel in the last `wheelQuietSeconds`, the lick's needs the spout released.
- Zapit (Pip, 13-14 Nov 2026): it adds no time; `laserToStimulusSeconds` is signed and its lead applies in every experiment; the ZapitTrigger line (DO3) is a held level, never board-pulsed (`outputPulseEnable: "DO1, DO2"`), raised at the trigger and cleared the Zapit file's max ramp-down after the outcome, only in optogenetics experiments, and cleared by the board piece however the workflow stops; no laser after an answer (`laserPowerMw` 0); the laser's cap, derived at Go, is at least the max response window (`responseWindowSeconds`) + the lead + 0.25 s. A rig without hardware triggering refuses optogenetics with any mouse but `test`.

### Audit 17 Nov 2026: not recommended

- Stage 2 now penalises a crossing of the zone floor that reaches the track's end within one 50 ms pip: left as is, as running about 16 cm in 50 ms is not physically plausible.

- `autoRewardProbability` triggers differently in each task: it is one concept, a free reward when the mouse does not earn one, given the way each task gives rewards.
