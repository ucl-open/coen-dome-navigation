## Decided

- The hardware is lib-open-rigs' `DomeHardware`; this workflow keeps its own task file reading, task C# (`src\Extensions\TrialLogic.cs`, the rules lib-open-rigs' `TrialLoop` runs, tested in `tests\Extensions.Tests`, every wait on the Harp clock) and `WorkflowData_StimulusState`. What every task does the same way is lib's (`TrialLoop`, and the includes `ReadLabRig`, `LaunchSettings`, `TrainingStage`, `BackgroundNoise`, `PlayPenaltySound`, `TaskLogs`, `LogRngSeed`, `ExperimentRecords`, `ConnectForTask`, `Rewards`); the pieces declare their own commands and parameters, so ExperimentControl declares only `TrainingStage`.
- The track's tone runs 2 to 24 kHz, as Tom's workflow did (Pip, 16 Nov 2026): his 'cm to Hz', drift and truncation divided `sweepEndHz / sweepStartHz` as whole numbers (25000 / 2000 = 12), so his mice learned the reward zones there (15248 Hz at 81.7% of the track). The 13 Nov rebuild had made it 2 to 25 kHz, moving every zone about 1.5% of the track earlier; `Sweep.TrackRatio` restores it. The tone files still span 2 to 25 kHz, so the file row uses the exact ratio, as Tom's did.
- The background noise is the lab's one file, rig.yml's `speakerArray.backgroundNoise` (`../shared/sounds/background_noise.wav`, this repo's old float WAV byte for byte; Pip: "It should be literally the same file"), from Ready, faded in over 2 s, at the task file's level as launched; Go stops if the form changed `backgroundNoise`.
- Every sound's level is the task file's `levelDbfs` (dBFS RMS as stored, before equalisation): pips -58.1, reward tone -55.1, background -64.1, penalty -44.6, the levels played before 15 Nov 2026. The penalty sound is the lab's, on rig.yml's `penaltySoundChannel`; the pips and reward tone play on rig.yml's speakers, each equalised.
- `sweep.json` is read at Start (`LoadSweep`), with the rate the waveforms were made at, checked against rig.yml's mixer rate.
- The quiet waits are per stage (`lickQuietSeconds`, `wheelQuietSeconds`, `wheelQuietCm`), capped at 10 s, 10 s and 20 cm; the wheel's counts travel in the last `wheelQuietSeconds`, the lick's needs the spout released.
- Zapit (Pip, 13-14 Nov 2026): it adds no time; `laserToStimulusSeconds` is signed and its lead applies in every experiment; the ZapitTrigger line (DO3) is a held level, never board-pulsed (`outputPulseEnable: "DO1, DO2"`), raised at the trigger and cleared `triggerHoldSeconds` after the outcome, only in optogenetics experiments, and cleared by the board piece however the workflow stops; no laser after an answer (`laserPowerMw` 0); `laserMaxSeconds` at least the longest response window (`responseWindowSeconds`) + the lead + 0.25 s. A rig without hardware triggering refuses optogenetics with any mouse but `test`.
- Rewards: 3 ul on every stage (Pip, 15 Nov 2026), planned from each valve's calibration; both valves need one (the manual `RewardValve-Right.Reward` uses the right).
- Wheel 75 mm (measured, 9 Nov 2026), rig.yml's `wheelDiameterMm`.
- The task file (Pip, 9 Nov 2026): `trainingStages` rows from 0 say what each stage does; `trialStructure` holds the interval (drawn evenly between `itiMinSeconds` and `itiMaxSeconds`, from the outcome, the 2 s to drink included) and track lengths; each stage's row its response window (`responseWindowSeconds`, from the first pip; null: it never closes).
- Trial types (Pip, 16 Nov 2026): the session-wide `modality` is gone; the task file's top-level `trialWeights: { visual, auditory, audiovisualCoherent, audiovisualConflict, neutral }`, relative integer weights in coen-dome-decision-making's names, are set per mouse on Setup and carried, so they survive stage advances; a `trainingStages` row may set its own (null by default), replacing them for that stage. All 0 and negatives are refused, and `audiovisualConflict` and `neutral` above 0 (no such trial defined here yet), in task.py and at Go. Each trial draws its type with the experiment's seeded draw (after its other draws) and shows the grating, the sweep or both; `trialType` in the trial row says which (`visual`, `auditory`, `audiovisualCoherent`; `audiovisual` until this change). The task file has `{ visual: 0, auditory: 1, ... }` and no stage of its own. `suggest-stage` reads the latest experiment whatever its trial types (no `--modality`).
- Trial log: `WorkflowData_Trials`, one row built at the outcome; `outcome` Correct/Incorrect/NoResponse; only what changes from trial to trial is a column (Pip, 9 Nov 2026). Tom's IntervalLog stays dropped: the Harp logs and `WorkflowData_StimulusState` (every 50 ms, row by row) replace it. The workflow's own logs (`WorkflowData\`) are one file per experiment; device logs one per clock hour.
- The Pupil camera's gain is 0 (rig.yml; Pip, 16 Nov 2026).
- Names (Pip, 17 Nov 2026): the task file is `config\task-abstract-corridor.yml`; the sound files are in `src\sounds\`, as every dome's; a stage has one number, the app's 0 to 5, and a `label:`; the 50 ms slot a pip may play in is a step (`StepSeconds`, `DriftSteps`, `firstPipStep`); `endReason` `FrequencyReached` and `TrackEnd`; the stimulus row is `StimulusStateRow`; the trial log's `penaltySoundOn`; the logs are `WorkflowData\WorkflowData_{Trials,Events,StimulusState}_<start>.csv`.
- swc-aeon is open-rigs' (lib-open-rigs), never pinned here; BSD 3-Clause, as lib-open-rigs.
- Recorded names changed (Pip, 16 Nov 2026; one name per meaning across the two dome tasks): the task file's `trainingStages[].timeLimitSeconds` is `responseWindowSeconds` (in Settings_Task), as coen-dome-decision-making's `trialStructure.responseWindowSeconds` and lib-open-rigs' `TrialLoop` response window; the `endReason` value `Timeout` is `ResponseWindowEnd` (in WorkflowData_Trials), since timeout means only the penalty's (`penalty.timeoutSeconds`, `penaltyTimeoutSeconds`). Earlier experiments keep the old names in their Settings_Task and manifest. In code, `CorridorOutcome` is `TrialOutcome`, as decision-making's.
- Not changed on audit: `outcomeTime` duplicates `harpTime` so every moment is named; laser timing names differ (`laserMaxSeconds` a cap, decision-making's `laserDurationSeconds` a timed stimulus, as lib-open-rigs' `LaserTiming`); the response window is per stage here (null on stages 0 to 2) and in `trialStructure` in decision-making (one for every stage); `trialWeights` (what each trial shows) and decision-making's `taskType` (what the mouse answers) are different settings; the update types keep their names (`StimulusFrame`, a pip; decision-making's `DecisionUpdate`, a lick's effect), as they are different things; `rngSeed` in the task file is what was asked, the `RngSeed` event what ran; a trial cut by Stop has no row; `rewardSizeUl` is a column though fixed, shared with decision-making's reader.
