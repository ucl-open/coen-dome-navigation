## Decided

- The hardware is lib-open-rigs' `DomeHardware`; this workflow keeps its own task file reading, task C# (`src\Extensions\TrialLogic.cs`, the rules lib-open-rigs' `TrialLoop` runs, tested in `tests\Extensions.Tests`, every wait on the Harp clock) and `WorkflowData_StimulusState`. What every task does the same way is lib's (`TrialLoop`, and the includes `ReadLabRig`, `LaunchSettings`, `TrainingStage`, `BackgroundNoise`, `PlayPenaltySound`, `TaskLogs`, `LogRngSeed`, `ExperimentRecords`, `ConnectForTask`, `Rewards`); the pieces declare their own commands and parameters, so ExperimentControl declares only `TrainingStage`.
- The track's tone runs 2 to 24 kHz, as Tom's workflow did (Pip, 16 Nov 2026): his 'cm to Hz', and drift divided `sweepEndHz / sweepStartHz` as whole numbers (25000 / 2000 = 12), so his mice learned the reward zones there (15248 Hz at 81.7% of the track). The 13 Nov rebuild had made it 2 to 25 kHz, moving every zone about 1.5% of the track earlier; `Sweep.TrackRatio` restores it. The tone files still span 2 to 25 kHz, so the file row uses the exact ratio, as Tom's did.
- The background noise is the lab's one file, rig.yml's `speakerArray.backgroundNoise` (`../shared/sounds/background_noise.wav`, this repo's old float WAV byte for byte; Pip: "It should be literally the same file"), from Ready, faded in over 2 s, at the task file's level as launched; Go stops if the form changed `backgroundNoise`.
- Every sound's level is the task file's `levelDbfs` (dBFS RMS as stored, before equalisation): pips -58.1, reward tone -55.1, background -64.1, penalty -44.6, the levels played before 15 Nov 2026. The penalty sound is the lab's, on rig.yml's `penaltySoundChannel`; the pips and reward tone play on rig.yml's speakers, each equalised.
- `sweep.json` is read at Start (`LoadSweep`), with the rate the waveforms were made at, checked against rig.yml's mixer rate.
- The quiet waits: `lickQuietSeconds` per stage, `wheelQuietSeconds` and `wheelQuietCm` in `trialStructure` (no stage changes them), capped at 10 s, 10 s and 20 cm; the wheel's counts travel in the last `wheelQuietSeconds`, the lick's needs the spout released.
- Zapit (Pip, 13-14 Nov 2026): it adds no time; `laserToStimulusSeconds` is signed and its lead applies in every experiment; the ZapitTrigger line (DO3) is a held level, never board-pulsed (`outputPulseEnable: "DO1, DO2"`), raised at the trigger and cleared `triggerHoldSeconds` after the outcome, only in optogenetics experiments, and cleared by the board piece however the workflow stops; no laser after an answer (`laserPowerMw` 0); `laserMaxSeconds` at least the longest response window (`responseWindowSeconds`) + the lead + 0.25 s. A rig without hardware triggering refuses optogenetics with any mouse but `test`.
- Rewards: 3 ul, the task file's `reward.rewardSizeUl` (Pip, 15 Nov 2026; in a `reward` block as decision-making's since 17 Nov), planned from each valve's calibration; both valves need one (the manual `RewardValve-Right.Reward` uses the right).
- Wheel 75 mm (measured, 9 Nov 2026), rig.yml's `wheelDiameterMm`.
- The task file (Pip, 9 Nov 2026; stages moved to the training pipeline 17 Nov 2026): the stage-set values are flat in `taskParameters` (stage 0's by default), rig-control writes the chosen pipeline stage's over them at launch and fills `trainingPipeline`, which Go checks against the Training Stage (0 to 99, the stage's id); `trialStructure` holds the interval (drawn evenly between `itiMinSeconds` and `itiMaxSeconds`, from the outcome, the 2 s to drink included) and track lengths; `responseWindowSeconds` the response window (from the first pip; null: it never closes).
- Trial conditions (trial-conditions step 2, 17 Nov 2026): `trialWeights` became `trialConditions: [{ type, weight, set }]`, as coen-dome-decision-making's (step 1): one whole value (`x-whole`), at least one condition, weights 0 or more with one above 0, types `visual`, `auditory`, `audiovisualCoherent`. The corridor has no condition fields yet: `x-conditionFields` is `{}` (kept, so app-rig-control reads both tasks one way) and every `set` must be empty; a field becomes condition-level by naming it there, as in decision-making. Each trial picks a condition by weight with the same draw as before (seeded runs unchanged) and logs `trialCondition`, its index, after the shared block; its description is decision-making's word for word, so the repeat sentence stands though the corridor never repeats. The task file's conditions are its old weights (visual 0, auditory 1, audiovisualCoherent 0), all three types kept so Setup, which edits only weights, can turn any on. main.bonsai is unchanged: with no condition fields there is nothing to build from the file's text at Go, so no `TaskText` subject until a field is added.
- Trial types (Pip, 16 Nov 2026; trialWeights since replaced by trialConditions, above): the session-wide `modality` is gone; the task file's top-level `trialWeights: { visual, auditory, audiovisualCoherent, audiovisualConflict, neutral }`, relative integer weights in coen-dome-decision-making's names, are set per mouse on Setup and carried, so they survive stage advances; a pipeline stage may set the whole map, replacing them for that stage. All 0 and negatives are refused, and `audiovisualConflict` and `neutral` above 0 (no such trial defined here yet), in task.py and at Go. Each trial draws its type with the experiment's seeded draw (after its other draws) and shows the grating, the sweep or both; `trialType` in the trial row says which (`visual`, `auditory`, `audiovisualCoherent`; `audiovisual` until this change). The task file has `{ visual: 0, auditory: 1, ... }`. `suggest-stage` reads the latest experiment whatever its trial types (no `--modality`).
- Trial log: `WorkflowData_Trials`, one row built at the outcome; `outcome` Correct/Incorrect/NoResponse; only what changes from trial to trial is a column (Pip, 9 Nov 2026). Tom's IntervalLog stays dropped: the Harp logs and `WorkflowData_StimulusState` (every 50 ms, row by row) replace it. The workflow's own logs (`WorkflowData\`) are one file per experiment; device logs one per clock hour.
- The Pupil camera's gain is 0 (rig.yml; Pip, 16 Nov 2026).

- swc-aeon is open-rigs' (lib-open-rigs), never pinned here; BSD 3-Clause, as lib-open-rigs.
- Names and removals (Pip, 17 Nov 2026; one name per meaning across the two dome tasks): in the task file `tonePips` is `auditoryStimulus`, the stage's `lickToReward` is `autoRewardProbability` (drawn per trial; 1 on stages 0 and 1), `truncatedTrialFraction` is `stimulusOffProbability`, the grating's constants are `visualStimulus.spatialFrequencyStartCpd` and `spatialFrequencyEndCpd`, and `DriftSteps` is `driftSeconds`; `trialStructure` has no coded default. `penaliseWrongZone` and `trackEndEndsTrial` are removed: an ending outside the zone is always penalised and the track's end always ends the trial (an auto-reward trial on the next pip, which reaches the floor). - Recorded names changed (Pip, 16 Nov 2026; one name per meaning across the two dome tasks): the task file's `trainingStages[].timeLimitSeconds` is `responseWindowSeconds` (in Settings_Task), as coen-dome-decision-making's `trialStructure.responseWindowSeconds` and lib-open-rigs' `TrialLoop` response window; the `outcomeReason` value `Timeout` is `ResponseWindowEnd` (in WorkflowData_Trials), since timeout means only the penalty's (`penalty.timeoutSeconds`, `penaltyTimeoutSeconds`). Earlier experiments keep the old names in their Settings_Task and manifest. In code, `CorridorOutcome` is `TrialOutcome`, as decision-making's.
- Outcome and stimulus-off names (Pip, 17 Nov 2026; one name per meaning): in WorkflowData_Trials `endReason` is `outcomeReason` (values unchanged), `endPositionCm` is `outcomePositionCm`, `auditoryEndFrequencyHz` is `outcomeAuditoryFrequencyHz` and `visualEndSpatialFrequencyCpd` is `outcomeVisualSpatialFrequencyCpd`, since "outcome" is the moment (`outcome`, `outcomeTime`); `truncationFraction` is two columns, `auditoryOffPositionCm` and `visualOffPositionCm`, the track position where that stimulus switched off (NaN when it never did), per modality so a later stage can cut one stimulus alone, and in cm since a sweep fraction meant nothing for the grating. Only stage 5 has stimulus-off trials and it has no drift, so its trials run as before; on a drift stage the cut-off follows position.
- Stage fields (Pip, 17 Nov 2026): "if it isn't chosen to be part of pipeline, then it should just be a global param like everything else". A setting sits on the `trainingStages` rows only if some stage changes it; otherwise it is task-level, under the same name. So `rewardSizeUl` moved to the top level and `wheelQuietSeconds` and `wheelQuietCm` to `trialStructure` (lib-open-rigs' `WheelQuietWait`); `lickQuietSeconds` (lib-open-rigs' `LickQuietWait`), `responseWindowSeconds`, `autoRewardProbability`, `rewardFloorHz`, `rewardCeilingHz`, `wrongLickLimit`, `trackLengthScale`, `frequencyDrift` and `stimulusOffProbability` stayed per stage. With the training pipeline (17 Nov 2026) the rows left the task file: these are flat in `taskParameters`, and the pipeline on the share says which each stage sets.
- Not changed on audit: `outcomeTime` duplicates `harpTime` so every moment is named; laser timing names differ (`laserMaxSeconds` a cap, decision-making's `laserDurationSeconds` a timed stimulus, as lib-open-rigs' `LaserTiming`); the response window is per stage here (null on stages 0 to 2) and in `trialStructure` in decision-making (one for every stage); `trialConditions` (what each trial shows) and decision-making's `taskType` (what the mouse answers) are different settings; the update types keep their names (`StimulusFrame`, a pip; decision-making's `DecisionUpdate`, a lick's effect), as they are different things; `rngSeed` in the task file is what was asked, the `RngSeed` event what ran; a trial cut by Stop has no row; `rewardSizeUl` is a column though fixed, shared with decision-making's reader.
- Gain and auto-progress (Pip, 17 Nov 2026; no aliases): the stage's `trackLengthScale` (0.125 on stages 0 and 1) is `wheelGain` (8 on stages 0 and 1, 1 after), track cm per wheel cm, and the track stays 80, 100 or 120 cm. The stage's `frequencyDrift` and the task's `driftSeconds` are one stage setting, `autoProgressPerSecond` (0.04 on stage 0, the whole track in 25 s; 0 after). The tone (nearest tone file) and grating are both set from one progress, wheel cm x `wheelGain` / track length + `autoProgressPerSecond` x seconds since the trial start, so they stay in step and stage 0 and 1's tone moves through about 98 tone files rather than 10 to 15 jumps; the track's end and the stimulus-off points are judged on the wheel alone. `positionStepCm` is removed: no position is rounded. The logs stay in wheel cm (`trackLengthCm` is the wheel distance to the end, track cm / `wheelGain`), and WorkflowData_Trials gains `wheelGain`.

## Considered, not changed

- Commit-subject style differs from coen-dome-decision-making: pushed history is never rewritten.

### Audit 17 Nov 2026: not recommended

- Stage 2 now penalises a crossing of the zone floor that reaches the track's end within one 50 ms pip: left as is, as running about 16 cm in 50 ms is not physically plausible.
- coen-dome-decision-making's schema allows a null `responseWindowSeconds`: it is the lab's shared field, and a null is refused loudly by its task.py and its Go check.
- `autoRewardProbability` triggers differently in each task: it is one concept, a free reward when the mouse does not earn one, given the way each task gives rewards.
- Old session summaries keep the old names: they are recorded data.

### Trial-conditions audit round 2 (18 Nov 2026): not recommended

- A condition with no `weight` reads as 0 at Go (the typed read), where coen-dome-decision-making refuses it from the text: CheckTask has no node tree here, and the schema app-rig-control checks at launch requires `weight`.
