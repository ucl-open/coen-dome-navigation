# coen-dome-navigation

Tom Childs' auditory (and visual) VR corridor task on the lab's open-rigs platform, run from rig-control. A head-fixed mouse runs on a wheel through a virtual track while a tone sweeps up in frequency (and a grating's spatial frequency changes) with its progress along the track: wheel cm times the stage's `wheelGain` over the track length, plus the stage's `autoProgressPerSecond` times the seconds since the trial start; it licks in the reward zone for a reward. The stage is a stage of the lab's Mouse Curriculum (`K:\config\curricula\coen-dome-navigation\task-abstract-corridor.conditions.yml`, edited on rig-control's Mouse Curriculum page): rig-control writes that stage's values over the task file's flat values (stage 0's) at launch and fills `curriculum: {id, code, version, path, label, sha256}`, the only record of the stage (the workflow has no stage parameter): Go stops the workflow when it is empty or malformed, and `TaskFile` reports its `code-version` (S03A-k7f2) as the metric `curriculumStage` (see `src\ucl_open_coen_dome_navigation\task.py`).

Converted on 9 Nov 2026 from `ucl-open/auditory-vr-corridor` (`main` b67a1f6 with `zapit-integration` 1782543), which stays as it is: Tom's original, read only. Rebuilt on 13 Nov 2026 on the lab fork's shared dome blocks, with the task in tested C# and every task wait on the Harp clock.

## Running an experiment

From rig-control: choose the rig and the mouse (launched by its lab ID, such as `TC001`), then this workflow and `config\task-abstract-corridor.yml`. The form shows the Training Stage, suggested from the mouse's earlier experiments by app-rig-control's Mouse Curriculum (the share's `K:\config\curricula`, edited on its Mouse Curriculum page): Tom's rules from `gen_session.py`, as its measures and criteria. What the trials show is the task file's `trialConditions`, as coen-dome-decision-making's: a list of `{ trialType, weight, pairs, set }`, one whole value. A curriculum stage's conditions replace the file's, and Setup edits only the weights, for that launch. Each trial picks a condition with the weights as relative chances, then one of its combinations, the task's values with the condition's `set` over them: any field but the session fields (the schema's `x-session-fields`, each with its reason in task.py `SESSION_FIELDS`: the seed, the curriculum, the mix, the background noise, the reward tone and penalty sound levels, and Zapit's connection, conditions file, laser cap and trigger hold, all fixed at Go), every list field (`contrasts`, `auditoryStimulus.initialAzimuthsDeg`, `trialStructure.trackLengthsCm`, `stimulusOff.trackFractions`, the set's or the task's) one entry per combination, at most `x-max-combinations` (1024) per condition, each trial reading every value from its combination, its row of the experiment's `Settings\Settings_Combinations_<time>.json` (written at Go: its condition, trialType, weight and the values that differ between combinations or from `Settings_Task`) logged as `trialCombinationId` (the schema's `x-rules` and `x-trial-types` are JSON Schema over dotted paths, evaluated by lib-open-rigs' `open_rigs.rigs.conditions` in pydantic, at check-workflow and by app-rig-control at launch; `x-required-conditions` is empty, the corridor having no target). Among them `contrasts` (the grating's, one drawn per trial; 0 no grating) and `auditoryStimulus.amplitude` (the pips' scale as stored, 1 the -58.1 dBFS RMS they have always played at; 0 no tone), `auditoryStimulus.initialAzimuthsDeg` (one picked per trial: `all`, the default, every rig.yml speaker; a number, rig.yml's speaker nearest it), `reward.rewardSizeUl`, `autoRewardProbability` and `optogenetics.laserOnProbability`, a list in a scalar cell one value per combination and `pairs` drawing listed values together (one index for each group). The `trialType` (`visual`, `auditory`, `audiovisualCoherent` or `neutral`, coen-dome-decision-making's words) names the condition and drives nothing: derived from its contrasts and amplitude unless a curriculum chooses another, logged as `trialType`, with the condition's 0-based index as `trialCondition` and what the trial showed as `visualContrast` and `auditoryAmplitude`. So a curriculum's path A can be visual (`auditoryStimulus.amplitude: 0`) and path B auditory (`contrasts: [0]`). The schema's `x-rules` (task.py `RULES`, checked by the model and at Go) only say what goes unread here: `visualStimulus`'s spatial frequencies at contrast 0, `wrongLickLimit` and `rewardCeilingHz` at `autoRewardProbability` 1, `stimulusOff` at `stimulusOffProbability` 0. All weights 0 is refused, and so is a condition that cannot be built (a field not in `x-conditionFields`, unequal paired lists, more than 64 combinations, a negative contrast or amplitude). Launch puts the rig in ready mode (hardware armed, cameras on, spouts homed, the background noise on from Ready at the launched task file's level, nothing recorded); Go reads the task file and starts the trials once the experiment folder exists.

What changed from Tom's workflow, and why, is in `BACKLOG.md` (Decided); the rig checks before the first optogenetics experiment are in `docs\zapit-rig-test.md`.

## Optogenetics

Zapit (from Tom's `zapit-integration-v2`, 9 Nov 2026), on with the task file's `optogenetics.enabled`:

- Every stimulus is continuous (no `StimDuration` or `StartDelay` is sent): Zapit ignores StopStimulus for a timed one. The laser runs from the trigger (`laserToStimulusSeconds` before the first pip, signed: after it if negative) until Bonsai stops it at the outcome, or at `laserMaxSeconds` on the Harp clock (a wall-clock backstop should the board stall).
- Every trial sends Zapit a condition drawn at random, laser-off trials at 0 mW, so galvo noise and masking light are the same on laser-on and laser-off trials; the first tone pip comes `laserToStimulusSeconds` after step 0 on every trial of the experiment.
- Timing: each trial's interval runs from the last outcome (after any timeout; trial 1 from the first clock tick). The trial's start (step 0) is the interval's end less the lead, max(`laserToStimulusSeconds`, 0), or later until the quiet waits hold (the stage's `lickQuietSeconds` with the spout released, a held contact holding the start; the wheel's travel, forward and back added, within `trialStructure.wheelQuietCm` over the last `wheelQuietSeconds`; each counted from the interval's start; `quietWaitSeconds` in the trial row says what they added). The first pip comes the lead after the trial start in every experiment, optogenetics on or off, so with a quiet mouse it lands exactly `itiSeconds` after the outcome. On a hardware-triggered rig Zapit is armed during the interval, once the last trial's trigger line has fallen, so it adds no time; the trigger must find it armed with the trial's condition and laser state, and a late reply, any other answer or any Zapit error reply stops the workflow. A trial that ends before its trigger has no laser and logs `laserPowerMw` 0.
- At connect Zapit's loaded condition count must equal the task's conditions file's, and each condition's `offRampDownDuration_ms` must be 100 to 500 ms.
- A rig without hardware triggering (rig.yml `zapit.hardwareTriggered`) runs optogenetics with the `test` mouse only: software start is tens of ms late, varies and has no onset in the Harp log.
- In an optogenetics experiment only, the trigger line (and the masking LEDs wired to it) rises at every trial's trigger (`laserToStimulusSeconds` before the first pip) and falls `triggerHoldSeconds` after the outcome; without optogenetics it stays low.
- Each trial row's `laserTriggerTime` is the trigger's Harp time, NaN without optogenetics or on a trial answered before its trigger, so a laser-off trial (a time, `laserPowerMw` 0) and one answered first (NaN) differ.

## Data

Each experiment is `C:\Coen-Lab-Local\Acquired-Data\sub-<lab ID>\ses-<session>_<UTC>`: the Harp boards' raw logs, the cameras (each `Camera-Face`, `Camera-Pupil` and `Camera-Body` folder holds an hourly `.mkv` (H.264) and a `.csv` of one row per frame: `harpTime`, `frameId`, `cameraClockNs`, `exposureUs`, `gainDb`; the encoder is `videoEncoder` in rig.yml), `WorkflowData\WorkflowData_Trials_*.csv` (one row per trial, columns in `schemas\trial-columns.yml`; `trialType` the condition's, `visualContrast` and `auditoryAmplitude` what it showed, `auditoryAzimuthDeg` and `auditoryElevationDeg` the one speaker the pips played on, NaN when they played on every speaker), `WorkflowData\WorkflowData_Events_*.csv` (rewards, commands, Go, and each Zapit reply as a `ZapitReply` row when optogenetics is on), `WorkflowData\WorkflowData_StimulusState_*.csv` (one row per 50 ms pip while a trial runs: `harpTime`, `trialNumber`, `positionCm`, `auditoryFrequencyHz`, `auditoryAmplitude` (the pip's amplitude: the trial's, 0 while a stimulus-off trial has the tone switched off), `visualSpatialFrequencyCpd`, `visualContrast` (the same for the grating: the trial's contrast, 0 once switched off)), each one file for the experiment, the settings files (`Settings_Rig`, `Settings_Task`, `Settings_Combinations`, `Settings_Experiment`, and with optogenetics `Settings_ZapitConditions`), each valve's calibration under `RewardValve-Left\` and `RewardValve-Right\` and `manifest.json`.

`uv run summarise-experiment <experiment folder>` reads one experiment from these files alone (lib-open-rigs' `open_rigs.rigs.trials`, tested there, the same for every dome task): how the run ended (the manifest), trials, performance, each response time and outcome-to-next-stimulus interval, and the water the task asked for against the water delivered. It also checks the rows against each other and names each problem, exiting 1: trial numbers with a gap, an outcome not `Correct`, `Incorrect` or `NoResponse`, a trial start or a stimulus more than 2 ms from where the task file puts it (`itiStartTime + itiSeconds + quietWaitSeconds`), a laser trigger more than 2 ms from `laserToStimulusSeconds` before the stimulus, an interval not starting at the last outcome plus its `penaltyTimeoutSeconds`, a `penalised` trial without the task's timeout and, when the task has it on, the penalty sound (or an unpenalised one with either), a `rewarded` trial with no `RewardDelivered` at its outcome requesting its `rewardOfferedUl`, or a `RewardDelivered` at the outcome of a trial not rewarded. A trial cut short by Stop has no trial row; it is said as unfinished, its outcome-to-next-stimulus interval taken from `WorkflowData_StimulusState`, and never counted as a problem.
